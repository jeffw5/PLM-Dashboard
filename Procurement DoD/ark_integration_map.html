RDSG Pillar 1 · SHACL/SWRL Validation

PSM Reliability Scoring & Circuit Breaker
PSM Pillar 3 · Brier Score · MTBH · Logarithmic
AI output is scored against strictly proper scoring rules (Brier, Logarithmic). If the hallucination rate exceeds the MTBH threshold, the circuit breaker fires — blocking the output from propagating to operators or into the SysML v2 repository. Compliant output advances.

SBD MOVOI Tradespace Analysis
SBD Pillar 2 · Multiobjective Value of Information
Scored AI context feeds SBD's MOVOI engine, which evaluates the full design tradespace across cost, schedule, performance, and risk. MOVOI answers "Is there enough information to commit to a design?" — if not, the tradespace is preserved and uncertainty-reduction activities are prioritized.

Ark Application Hub Interaction
Govini Ark · Supply Chain · Production · Modernization

Ontology Gap Detection
RDSG · Real-Time Session Monitoring
As operators work in Hubs, RDSG monitors for terms the AI fails to resolve. Unresolved concepts are automatically captured as OntologyGap individuals — tagged with the operator's persona, scenario, and program context — ready for steward remediation without interrupting workflow.

Automated Requirements Review
RDSG · Steward-Triggered Governance Calibration
When AI failures cross an Acceptance Criterion threshold, the Hub surfaces a RequirementsReview directly to domain stewards. Stewards assess whether the gap reflects a requirements deficiency, ontology error, or AI capability limit — then calibrate accordingly.
