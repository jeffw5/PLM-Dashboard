Context · Step 2 of 9

Jobs-to-Be-Done Elicitation

Define the FunctionalJob this requirement set governs. Structure: When [situation], I want to [job], so I can [outcome]. Each job requires a 4-type Unsafe Control Action pre-analysis to seed the v2.0 UCA Profile loaded by Stage 0 of the gateway pipeline.

FunctionalJob Definition
SSE homeostatic feedback — describe how this FunctionalJob is regulated (not merely optimized) against the Right Value Anchor selected in Step 1. This informs the Generation Governance Envelope in Step 3.
UCA Pre-Analysis — 4 Unsafe Control Action Types (RDSG v2.0)
For each UCA type: describe what constitutes a hazardous control action for this FunctionalJob. These seed the UCA Profile at Stage 0 and activate TMP-001–006 and UCA-001–005 SWRL rules at Step 3.
Missing — Not Providing
What hazard occurs if the AI agent fails to provide output (e.g. times out, returns nothing)?
Hazardous — Providing When Should Not
What hazard occurs if the AI provides output in a wrong or unauthorized context?
MisTimed — Too Early / Too Late / Wrong Order
What hazard occurs if the AI provides output at the wrong time or out of the required sequence?
WrongDuration — Stopped Too Soon / Applied Too Long
What hazard occurs if the AI stops output too early or continues beyond its authorized scope?

Quality & Decomposition · Step 8 of 9 — Stage 1B

NLP Quality Gate

All requirements are evaluated against INCOSE Guide v4 quality characteristics, 8 NLP anti-pattern detectors, and a 9th SSE dimension — Socio-Technical Alignment. Violation-severity findings block advancement to SHACL validation. Warning-severity findings are advisory with steward override capability. All findings are typed NLPFinding OWL individuals with affected text, INCOSE characteristic citation, and an LLM-generated rewrite suggestion. Runs entirely on-device in air-gap mode with no external API calls (Addendum C).

🌐SSE Run-Time Threshold Indicator. Socio-Technical Alignment findings are the operational form of the "socio-technical threshold indicator" the SSE Integration Proposal calls for — a testable check, not a normative guideline, evaluated on every requirement set that reaches this gate.
Click Run NLP Quality Gate to evaluate requirements against INCOSE Guide v4 and NLP anti-pattern detectors.
Approval · Step 9 of 9

Review & Approve

Review the complete requirement set. Approve to activate in the triplestore (status → Validated). Revise to return to Draft with revision notes. Reject to exclude from triplestore activation. Approved requirements seed the gateway pipeline RCB on the next interaction and become available to the DSM Materialization Service (Addendum B).
