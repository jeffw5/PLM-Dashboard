Transformation thesis

From Fragmented Systems to a Governed Trust Fabric

Evolve ISACA's membership, certification, learning and community operations onto one governed digital platform — applying the same discipline the Association teaches (COBIT, CMMI, NIST AI RMF) to its own transformation, so every credential, CPE record and framework interaction is traceable, verifiable and instantly trusted by members, employers and regulators alike.

Reference architecture

A six-layer stack, one governance plane

Six functional layers plus a cross-cutting governance plane, each with a defined interface contract with its neighbors. The design is member- and credential-first: every certification, competency, CPE record and framework interaction has a formal definition in a shared model before analytics, personalization or AI ever touch it. Select a layer for its component inventory, technologies, considerations and key decisions.

Design principles

Phased transformation

Five phases, one continuous trust thread

Each phase adds capability without re-platforming the last, and concludes with a formal Governance Gate Review — a go / no-go decision, judged the way ISACA judges any enterprise IT investment, requiring evidence of delivery, adoption and governance readiness before the next phase is funded. Select a phase for its workstreams, gate and investment.
