Built from the same taxonomy-core-persona/ ontology as the Elena Vance operator instance, populated here for a job-to-be-done the smart-mobility literature almost never models directly: getting an ordinary resident to work. Facts marked from source are drawn from documented reporting on the Smart Columbus initiative (kiosks used on the order of 8 trips/unit/year; an autonomous shuttle capped near 4 mph and withdrawn after a low-speed collision), the Sidewalk Labs Toronto data-consent collapse, and Flock Safety license-plate-camera misuse (officer stalking, warrantless protest monitoring) — the same source material examined in the accompanying S.E.P.A. gap-analysis document. Fields marked illustrative (name, email, phone, exact address, employer, specific timestamps) have no basis in that reporting and are placeholders, consistent with the ontology's own definition of a persona as “a semi-fictional representation.” Dashed cards/rings are taxonomy classes with no populated individual in this instance — several are left unpopulated deliberately, because no personalization loop, feedback channel, or consent-selection mechanism existed in the deployments this persona is modeled on; that absence is itself the finding.
