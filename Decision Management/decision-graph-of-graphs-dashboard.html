Inter-Domain Frame

Domains sense change both intra-domain (within their own boundary) and inter-domain (horizontal peers, vertical strategy/execution flow). Edges below represent dependency & impact, not just data flow.

Value Chain Frame

Once decisions cross a company boundary, the receiving company's own domain graph (Step 1) is opaque to you by default — you only see the interface: what was decided, what's expected in return.

Ecosystem Frame

3rd parties are heterogeneous: individuals, automated agents, other companies, and institutions each decide under different authority and incentive structures. Model each as its own decision-capable node.

Decisions poorly implemented

Sense → Analyze → Respond → Learn

Sense intra/inter-domain signals → Analyze against catalog/models/analytics → Respond with a decision (forward/backward chaining) → Learn and harvest the pattern for reuse. This loop runs inside every stratum.

Graph of Graphs

Each ring is itself a graph whose nodes are, one level in, graphs of their own — a containment hierarchy that still allows lateral edges within a ring (peer domains, peer companies, peer 3rd parties).
