Chapter 0

How it fits together

Every desired outcome has an importance score, a satisfaction score and an opportunity score for PEO clients and for employers without a PEO, and it links to the services that deliver it. The same data answers three questions, and what the field learns flows back into the next study.

Chapter 1

Build battle cards from outcomes

A battle card speaks in the buyer's success measures, not in product features. One card per persona and theme keeps it short enough to use on a call.

  1. Pick the persona and theme

    Start with the buyer (usually the Owner, Finance Leader or HR Leader) and one theme. Use the Theme Explorer to see which jobs and outcomes it covers.

  2. Choose the audience

    For prospects, rank needs by the non-PEO scores. For renewals and expansion, rank by PEO-client scores.

  3. Lead with unmet needs

    Take the outcomes with the highest opportunity for that audience. These are the problems the buyer already feels.

  4. Add proof only where it is significant

    A proof point needs PEO clients to be better served by more than the 95% interval on the gap. Quote satisfaction as a share of respondents and cite the outcome ID and study wave.

  5. Turn needs into discovery questions

    Let the prospect describe the gap. The tool drafts a question from each outcome; edit it into the rep's own voice.

  6. Prepare for weak spots

    Where PEO clients are less served, prepare a candid answer and point to the improvement under way. Never hide it.

  7. Send it through review

    Compliance clears each statement's approved uses in PEO Outcome Review. Only outcomes approved for external marketing claims can appear in external material.

Chapter 2

Drive continuous improvement and innovation

Comparing each outcome's opportunity for PEO clients with its opportunity for employers without a PEO tells you what kind of work it needs. Themes are the business model's service lines, so every action already has an owner.

  1. Classify every outcome

    Fix PEO clients are under-served and worse off than the alternative. Innovate Everyone is under-served. Protect Our advantage; keep it and market it. Simplify Satisfaction exceeds importance for PEO clients. Maintain Everything else.

  2. Route to the owner

    Each outcome's theme names the service line; its services lead to the business process and operating team in the ontology.

  3. Define the measure

    Outcomes are already measurable. Pair each fix with an operating metric (late-payroll rate, days to resolve a tax notice) so progress shows between survey waves.

  4. Score new ideas by the need they close

    A concept's score is the opportunity above the threshold for each outcome it addresses, times how much of the gap it would close. Compare concepts on that score, not on opinion.

  5. Re-measure

    A short pulse on the top outcomes between full studies shows whether a fix moved the score.

Chapter 3

Guide sales staff with an AI Sales Advisory Agent

The agent works from the outcome graph and the study results, not from open-ended text. It retrieves the outcomes that match what the prospect said, then advises the rep. It never states a number it did not retrieve, and a rep approves anything before a client sees it.

1 · ProfileWho is this?

Persona, size, industry, states and current HR model, from CRM and the conversation.

2 · RetrieveWhat matters to them?

Query the graph for that persona's jobs and the outcomes ranked by opportunity for their segment.

3 · AdviseWhat should the rep do?

Next question, significant proof point with its source, candid answer to a weak spot, the right specialist.

4 · LogWhat did we learn?

Outcomes raised and win or loss go back to CRM, feeding the next study and the backlog.

Chapter 4
Governance and cadence

  
The same rules hold for cards, backlog and agent, so a claim means the same thing wherever it appears.

  

    
A claim needs a significant gap
Only cite an advantage when the PEO vs non-PEO gap is outside its 95% interval, and always cite the outcome ID and study wave.

    
Use only approved uses
Compliance marks each outcome for survey, internal sales guidance and AI agent, or external marketing claims in PEO Outcome Review.

    
Be candid about weak spots
Where PEO clients are less served, sales says so and names the improvement. It builds trust and keeps the backlog honest.

    
No advice outside the lane
Neither cards nor the agent give legal, tax or insurance advice. Those questions go to a licensed specialist.

    
Aggregate, never individual
Study results and conversation logs are reported in groups of at least five, consistent with the model's privacy safeguards.

    
A person approves
Agent output is advice to a rep. Nothing it drafts reaches a client without the rep's review.

  

  

  

    
Each study waveRefresh everything
Load results, regenerate cards, re-sort the action map, update the agent's data.

    
QuarterlyPulse and backlog review
Pulse the top 30 outcomes; service-line owners report on fixes and innovation bets.

    
MonthlyField learning
Review outcomes raised in calls and win/loss by outcome; add new outcomes to the next study.

    
On changeCompliance re-review
Any new wording or new use for a statement goes back through PEO Outcome Review.
