Method and data notes

Scoring. Importance and satisfaction are the share of respondents rating an outcome 4 or 5 on a 5-point scale, divided by 10. Opportunity = I + max(I − S, 0). Tiers follow the landscape guide embedded in the study workbook: above 15 extreme, 12–15 high, 10–12 solid, below 10 limited. An outcome is over-served when satisfaction exceeds importance.

Data alignment. In the workbook's Adult tab, the Pediatric I/S/O columns are each sorted by pediatric rank, so on 227 of 232 rows they belong to a different outcome than the Adult statement beside them. This view joins Adult and Pediatric scores by outcome statement instead. The workbook's Adult-vs-Pediatric landscape chart is unaffected, since each plotted point keeps its own I/S pair.

Sample size. Pediatric results rest on 35 respondents, so one respondent moves a score by about 0.3. The 95% intervals shown for opportunity assume independent importance and satisfaction estimates. Total N is 334, of whom 30 are not in either the Adult or Pediatric group.

Themes and metric types. Themes are a keyword-based coding of each outcome statement's wording (for example allergy, dose, incompatibility → medication and dose safety). They group statements for analysis and are not survey questions in their own right.
