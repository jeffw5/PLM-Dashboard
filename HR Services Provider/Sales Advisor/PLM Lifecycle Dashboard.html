    Assumptions behind the plan

    • All scorecard components and all six portfolios are built from scratch; no existing practice is reused without being rebuilt to the new standard.
    • Durations are in weeks for a dedicated core team per workstream; portfolio teams join at their wave. Each portfolio’s Discover & Define overlaps the previous wave so the next team learns from it.
    • Buffers total , sized by novelty and regulatory exposure: 2 weeks to mobilize, 4 for foundations, 6 for the pilot and wave 2, 8 for in-service support (DO-178C/DO-254, FAA/EASA), 4 for the final wave.
    • The Digital Supply Chain is the pilot because it is the only portfolio with assessment data and it feeds every later wave (serialized parts, supplier quality, spares).
    • Dates start on the first Monday of November 2026 and are planning dates, not commitments.
