PEO Sales Advisor

A governed advisor that pulls content from SharePoint, public websites and third-party feeds, turns it into RDF on the Keel, and assembles it into briefs for the Client Admin and HR Specialist as they move through the sales pipeline. Every brief cites its sources, every decision is recorded with the outcome it commits to, and every upstream change is traced to the jobs and decisions it affects.

The Keel holds what must stay true: ontologies, the SSOT extraction rules, the RDF content graph and the mapping graph. The North Star is what personas act on: assembled briefs, recorded decisions, orchestrated events and the measured outcomes that show whether the advisor helped. Seven agent families run between them.

Solid arrows carry content and queries. Dashed arrows carry change signals and measurements back to the Keel, where they become KGCL change requests or causal findings.

Each source item passes the same seven stages. Identify gives it a canonical URI, Classify and Enrich use the AI fabric to mine metadata the CMS never held (personas, job steps, claims, jurisdictions, validity dates), Control applies access and privacy rules from the SSOT, and Publish writes RDF only after SHACL validation. Pick a source item and run it.

Connectors and change capture by source

Agents are grouped by family. Each one reads only what its rules allow, writes only to its declared target, and carries an agent identity with an autonomy level. Faceted search runs as a chain: federated graph query → mappings → RDF content → precision extraction → assembly.

Federated graph query→Mappings (GOV-006)→RDF content structures→Precision extraction→Assembly for human or machine

The advisor at work. Pick a persona question: watch the faceted search chain run, read the assembled brief with its citations and access labels, then choose a decision. The decision is recorded against a desired outcome and emits orchestrated events, and the advisor shows which later decisions it changes.

Extraction, control and assembly rules live in the SSOT register as EXT- rules, in the same 14-field schema as the policy rules. Each is authored once as an LKIF norm plus SHACL shape, compiled by GOV-019, and promoted through DEV/STG/PRD (GOV-003) before any agent may load it. Agents pin a rule version; a new version reaches them only after it passes every check.

Value-gap monitors compare the outcome each persona wants at a job step with what the advisor actually delivered. Desired outcomes are written as measurable statements ("minimize the time it takes to…") and bound to Metric Definition Cards, the same way MET-014 binds decisions. Change monitors watch the semantic layer, the mappings, the RDF structures and the source repositories. When something changes, the impact agent finds every assembly, persona job and in-flight decision that depends on it, the refresh service updates only those parts, and the causal agent estimates the risk or opportunity it creates.

Four gated phases over 15 months on the Keel and North Star architecture. Keel work comes first in each phase: domain knowledge and ontologies, then RDF structures for content, then mappings. North Star work follows on what the Keel has released. No phase opens until its gate passes.
