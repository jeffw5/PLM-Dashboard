Measurement register

Every measure has one owner, the stage where it is taken, the PLM gate it gives evidence to, and the scores it feeds. Thresholds live in the SSOT as constraints, so a threshold change goes through the same Draft → Candidate → Approved → Released workflow as any rule.

How measures become PLM scores

Each asset version and component version is a PLM item. Measures attach to it as gate evidence. All three scores roll up the business operating model, the same graph the impact tracer walks, so a business service shows its compliance, its risk and its projected impact side by side.

Compliance

Share of conformance checks passed against keel rules, standards, controls and contracts, weighted by rule severity in the SSOT. Computed per asset or component version, then rolled up by process, service and domain.

Risk

Severity × likelihood of open findings: drift, escaped defects, shadow metrics, integrity-signal failures, breaking tool changes and reviewer overrides. Open findings feed the PLM defect burndown and stay on the gate until closed.

Impact

Projected and realized value traced from the asset through processes, services and domains to products, strategy objectives and values. Every figure carries its determinism ratio, so projected paths are never reported as realized.

Causal chain, summarized by agents

The business operating model lives in the keel, so the graph that supplies governance inputs also carries the impact paths. The first four links are populated from the SSOT and the agent registry today. The last three bind once Insperity's product catalog, strategy map and values are loaded as keel assets (K12).

Guardrails on the summary

  • Each link in a path is labelled: declared in the register, carried by a consumer or referenced rule, or a proposed mapping. The summary reports the determinism ratio of every path it cites.
  • A path that uses any proposed mapping is reported as projected, never as realized. Deterministic propagation over ungated inference is blocked by rule.
  • The summarization agent runs behind the AI Circuit Breaker. Claims that don't cite an asset ID, a path and a measure fail validation and route to a reviewer.
  • Results move a PLM item through G5 only after human sign-off, with author and approver kept separate (CTL-013).
  • Values and strategy objectives also flow down as constraints: an asset that improves a measure while breaching a value-linked norm shows positive impact and an open compliance finding side by side.
