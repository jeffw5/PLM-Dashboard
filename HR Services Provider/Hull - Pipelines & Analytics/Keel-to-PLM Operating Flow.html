Measurement register

Every measure has one owner component, the stage where it is taken, the PLM gate it gives evidence to, and the scores it feeds. Thresholds live in the SSOT as constraints, so a change to a threshold goes through the same Draft → Candidate → Approved → Released workflow as any rule.

How measures become PLM scores

Each component version is a PLM item. Measures attach to it as gate evidence. The three scores roll up along the same business-model graph that carries impact, so a strategy objective shows its projected value next to the compliance and risk of every component behind it.

Compliance

Share of conformance checks passed against keel rules, standards and contracts, weighted by rule severity in the SSOT. Computed per component version, then rolled up by holon.

Risk

Severity × likelihood of open findings: drift, escaped defects, shadow metrics, circuit-breaker trips, reviewer overrides. Open findings feed the PLM defect burndown and stay attached to the gate until closed.

Projected and realized value propagated from the component through operational services and products to strategy objectives and values. Every figure carries its determinism ratio, so estimated paths are never reported as realized.

Causal impact, summarized by agents

The business model lives in the keel, so the graph that supplies governance inputs also carries the impact paths. When a component version changes or its measures move, the causal engine traces it upward and an agent writes the assessment for each audience.

The example row follows one change through the chain. Its names are illustrative placeholders; real paths come from the client's business model in the keel.

Guardrails on the summary

  • Each edge in a path is marked curated (asserted in the keel, approved) or estimated (inferred by an agent). The summary reports the determinism ratio of every path it cites.
  • A path with any estimated edge is reported as projected, never as realized. Deterministic propagation over ungated inference is blocked by rule.
  • The summarization agent runs behind the AI Circuit Breaker. Claims that don't cite a path ID and its measures fail validation and route to a reviewer.
  • Results move a PLM item through G5 only after human sign-off, with author and approver kept separate.
  • Values and strategy objectives also flow down as constraints: a component that improves a metric while breaching a value-linked norm shows positive impact and an open compliance finding side by side.
