Solution architecture · three Insperity demos on one semantic fabric

Keel and North Star Architecture

The three demos are not three products. They are three views from the deck of one vessel. Each is held upright by the same keel of governed meaning and ballast of governance, and each steers by the same North Star. Two core capabilities on the bridge, Compass and Copilot, read that keel to create every experiential feature. Start with how the keel enables them, then see each demo’s features, then trace any path through the vessel.

Keel, Compass, Copilot

The keel holds meaning. Compass and Copilot hold none of their own: they read it through the hull. Compass is how people find and understand meaning; Copilot is how AI answers and acts on it, and it grounds every phrase through Compass’s index before it speaks.

Core capability · people-led

Compass

Company-wide search and navigation, steering by the North Star. It finds things by meaning, shows how they connect, and explains where every number comes from.

  • Search resolves words to concepts, not keywords
  • Browse by holon: domain → context → service → concept
  • “Explain this number” down to card, rules, calc and URI
  • Shows only what the user’s role and tenant allow
Core capability · AI-led

Copilot

Insperity’s HR360 agent strategy and conversational reporting, with Microsoft 365 Copilot as another front door. It answers, drafts and proposes, but only on grounded meaning.

  • Decomposes every request (GOV-011) before acting
  • Grounds each phrase through Compass’s index, or trips the breaker
  • Quotes version-pinned numbers with a URI
  • Drafts changes; humans approve anything irreversible (GOV-015)
