PEO Intelligence Fabric

An intelligence platform for a two-person CI team, modeled on what Nestlé, Disney and the largest PEOs run. AI works only on knowledge grounded in the keel and in vetted third-party data, and the AI Circuit Breaker scores quality, relevancy and trust at every step from input to published card.

Inputs → processing → outputs, with a deterministic keel underneath and a Circuit Breaker gate after every stage. Internal PEO knowledge and third-party data enter on the left; nothing reaches a battlecard, a brief or an AI answer on the right without passing six gates.

Pick a signal and run it. Each gate scores Quality, Relevancy and Trust from 0 to 100. The lowest score sets the verdict: Pass at 75 or more, Hold for a person at 60–74, Trip below 60, which stops everything downstream. Corrected means a deterministic fix from the keel was applied first.

Decision traceability, extended to the source. The Insights demo traced insight → decision → outcome → measured result. Here every insight also traces back to the signals behind it, and each signal carries its six-gate lineage. Rule MET-014 still requires an outcome bound to a Released metric card, and a decision built on a tripped signal is stopped before it is recorded.

Core taxonomies are shared by every domain: parties, offerings, segments, sources, signals, trust and decisions. Domain taxonomies specialize them for competitive intelligence, PEO services, regulation, client metrics and AI governance. Every concept carries alternate labels, a definition, mappings to SSOT rules and ontology classes, and the fine-tuning roles it can play.

Two people, eight agents. Agents collect, tag, verify, correlate, draft and keep records. People set the rules, review holds, approve new vocabulary and own every decision and every publish. No agent can act past its tier, and every agent passes through the same gates as the pipeline.

L

Lead · Director of Competitive Intelligence

Owns decisions, sets thresholds and freshness windows, approves new taxonomy concepts, publishes outside the team (A3). About 18 hours a week back from collection and upkeep.

A

Analyst · CI & insights

Reviews holds, audits samples of agent work, writes analysis and briefs, runs win/loss interviews. About 17 hours a week back for analysis and coverage.
