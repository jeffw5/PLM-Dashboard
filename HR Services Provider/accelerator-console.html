Six accelerators, one governance spine

Insperity runs a co-employment model at scale — one company standing behind payroll, benefits, and compliance for thousands of client businesses and 300,000+ worksite employees. That only works if the rules governing every one of those relationships are written once, enforced consistently, and traceable end to end. That's what these six accelerators are built to do.

Each panel below is a working prototype of an accelerator I've built and refined in enterprise settings, re-cast against the priorities Insperity is actively investing in: platform modernization, an AI-first roadmap, and a widening product portfolio. Click any circuit — on the panel at left, or the cards below — to open it.

How the six connect

HSGA

Holonic Semantic Governance Architecture

Governance modeled as nested holons — each layer a whole in its own right and a part of the layer above it, autonomous within its scope but accountable to the obligations it inherits.

A PEO's co-employment model is holonic whether or not anyone names it that way: Insperity sits inside federal and state regulatory frameworks, each client company sits inside Insperity's policy envelope, and each worksite employee sits inside their client's plan choices. HSGA makes those nested scopes explicit and machine-checkable, so a rule authored once at the top is inherited correctly at every layer below — instead of re-implemented, and re-drifted, four separate times.

SSOT

rules, constraints & logic — authored once

One semantic source for policy — obligations, permissions, and prohibitions — compiled down into whatever a system actually needs: a live compliance check, or an analytics filter.

Expand a rule to see both forms: the canonical statement an SME or legal reviewer can read, and the compiled form a runtime system actually executes. Author once, enforce everywhere, and the same registry that runs the check also answers "how many obligations are we carrying, and where."
