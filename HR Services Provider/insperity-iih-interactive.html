    Governance & trust metrology

    Four-fold governance, and autonomy earned in stages

    Semantic, AI, regulatory/compliance and identity governance operate as one plane — and every Jarvis response is logged with full lineage back to the plan clause or statute that produced it.     Trust maturity ladder autonomy is earned, not granted

    Jarvis does not arrive autonomous. It climbs a gated ladder — each phase widens the Circuit Breaker's envelope only after the holarchy proves itself on the five MTBH trust dimensions (WHO · WHAT · WHEN · WHERE · WHY) and clears a reliability gate. Promotion is a governance decision, not a configuration flag.

    Domain holons · six practice areas

    One reference pattern, replicated six times

    Every domain holon is instantiated from the same five-component pattern — ontology, agent, hybrid vector database, PEPs with LKIF norms, and an MTBH monitor. Each is sovereign and self-governing, so adding the sixth never touches the first. Sites — practice domains — mature at their own pace as they come online through the roadmap.
