How this maps to SEBoK & the agents

The console operationalises the SEBoK Enterprise SE knowledge area (v2.2) and a rule-based multi-agent layer.

The gate = Enterprise Evaluation & Assessment

EE&A should look for break points where capabilities are significantly enhanced or totally disabled. A critical artifact at zero is that break point.

Target vs assessment = capability gap

Capability-Based Planning compares desired capability to the baseline. Targets are the desired profile; each project's assessment is the observed one.

Per-phase appetite = satisficing threshold

Each phase carries its own Conservative / Balanced / Lenient bar, applied to its cumulative-target maturity. Enterprise decisions satisfice, so the threshold is a choice, not a proof.

The agents

Each assessment agent is a deterministic advisor for one discipline lens — the in-browser proxy for the skill-library evaluators. It reads scores, rates likelihood × impact, and explains the consequence of proceeding unmitigated. Ratings are heuristic decision support, not a guarantee.
